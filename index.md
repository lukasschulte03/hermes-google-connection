# Nora Hermes Assistant

A private, self-hosted personal assistant for its installation owner and an invited
user. This is not a public signup service or a Google product.

The optional Google connection lets an authorised user ask the assistant to find
and summarise Gmail messages, compose or send messages, and review or manage Google
Calendar events. Actions are subject to the user's instructions. The connection
requests Gmail access, event access, and read-only access to the calendar list.
Google Drive, documents, contacts and spreadsheet access are not requested.

Google account access is approved separately through Google's OAuth consent
screen. Connecting an account does not publish its messages or calendar.

[Privacy and Google data handling](privacy.html)

For account access, data deletion or installation questions, contact the person
who invited you to this private assistant. Public technical information is kept
in the [installation information repository](https://github.com/lukasschulte03/hermes-google-connection).
Do not post email contents, tokens or other private account information in public
GitHub issues.
